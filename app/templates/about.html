Our Mission

Why we built this

At TechFixAI, our mission is to bridge the communication gap between frontline factory workers and IT support teams. Traditional ticket systems require technical literacy and manual entry — creating friction, delays, and lost context. We've built an AI system that lets workers simply speak naturally in Japanese, and handles everything else: transcription, translation, issue classification, severity scoring, and routing to the right developer — all in one automated pipeline.
