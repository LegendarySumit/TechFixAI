About TechFixAI

Transforming multilingual technical support with intelligent voice-to-ticket automation

Our Mission

We believe that language barriers shouldn't impact technical support quality. TechFixAI leverages cutting-edge AI and machine learning to instantly convert voice problems (especially Japanese) into structured, actionable support tickets. Our goal is to make support teams more efficient, reduce response times, and eliminate miscommunication.

Why We Built This

Problem Identification

Global support teams struggle with multilingual inputs, ticket categorization, and proper assignment of complex issues.

Our Solution

An AI-powered pipeline that transcribes, translates, structures, and routes support tickets automatically.

The TechFixAI Pipeline

1. Transcribe

Japanese audio is transcribed using Groq/OpenAI Whisper API with high accuracy.

2. Translate

Technical context is preserved during translation from Japanese to clear, actionable English.

3. Route

Structured tickets are intelligently assigned to developers based on expertise and availability.
