Commercial Policy

Billing & Compliance

Clear terms for subscriptions, refunds, taxes, and invoicing so finance and engineering teams can plan with confidence.

Cancellation

Subscriptions can be cancelled before the next billing cycle. Access remains active until the current billing period ends unless otherwise stated in a contract addendum.

Refunds

Refund requests are reviewed case-by-case for duplicate charges or billing errors. Usage-based charges are generally non-refundable once processing has occurred.

Tax & Invoicing

Invoices include mandatory tax fields for active sales geographies. Customers must provide accurate billing and tax information to maintain compliant invoicing records.

Compliance Scope

Commercial terms apply to all paid plans and are interpreted together with the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy for data handling and legal obligations.
