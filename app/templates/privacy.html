Introduction

TechFixAI ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform.

By using TechFixAI, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. We never sell your personal data.
02

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly and information generated through use of the platform:

  • Account information — name, email, username, and hashed passwords
  • Audio files — uploaded voice reports used for transcription and ticket generation
  • Ticket data — AI-generated transcriptions, translations, and issue classifications
  • Usage data — feature interactions, session timestamps, and access logs
03

How We Use Your Data

We use collected data solely to provide and improve the TechFixAI service:

  • Process audio files and generate structured support tickets
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions
  • Provide dashboard analytics and reporting to managers
  • Improve AI transcription and classification accuracy
  • Send system notifications and operational emails
04

Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary. Audio files are deleted 30 days after processing. Ticket data is retained for the life of your account. You may request deletion at any time.

05

Data Security

We implement AES-256 encryption at rest, TLS 1.3 for data in transit, and role-based access controls. However, no system is 100% secure and we encourage strong unique passwords.

06

Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable law, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. You may also request data portability or restrict processing.

07

Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or to exercise your rights, contact us at privacy@techfixai.com. We respond within 48 hours.
