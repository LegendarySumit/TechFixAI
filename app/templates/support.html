Common Questions

Frequently asked questions

What audio formats are supported?
TechFixAI accepts .wav and .mp3 files. For best results, use clear audio recorded in a quiet environment. The maximum file size is 25 MB.
How long does ticket processing take?
Most audio files are transcribed and converted into a structured ticket within 10–30 seconds, depending on file length and current load.
Can I re-assign a ticket after creation?
Yes. Managers can re-assign any ticket to a different developer from the ticket detail view at any time.
How does the AI classify issue severity?
The Groq LLM analyzes the translated text and assigns a severity level (Low / Medium / High / Critical) based on the nature and urgency of the reported problem.
Is my audio data stored permanently?
No. Audio files are automatically deleted 30 days after processing. Transcriptions and ticket data are retained for the life of your account. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
How do I add a new developer to the team?
Go to the Developers page and click Add Developer. Fill in their name and specialisation, and they will immediately be available for ticket assignment.
